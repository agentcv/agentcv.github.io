About the author

Hi, I'm John DuPre, a seasoned product leader. I built agentCV for my own job search.

Why agentCV exists

In 2026 I found myself looking for my next role. Job searching is a full-time job with terrible pay, so I did what product people do: I treated it like a product. I wrote down how a great recruiting agency would work for a single client, then set up an AI agent to run it, with me as the client.

The agent drafts. I review, edit and approve, and nothing goes out without me. It worked well enough (about double the typical callback rate) that people started asking for it, so I took my personal data out and shared it.
